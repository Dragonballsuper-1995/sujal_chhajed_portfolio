# Frontend Design

Approach this as the design lead at a design studio known for giving every client a distinct visual identity that is not mistaken for anyone else's. Make deliberate, opinionated choices about palette, typography, and layout that are specific to this brief, and take aesthetic risk if justified.

## Principles:
- Ground designs in subject matter (Sujal's AI/ML & Full-Stack portfolio, neo-brutalist theme).
- Typography carries personality: bold neo-brutalist hierarchy (Archivo Black + JetBrains Mono), solid boundary plates.
- Structure is information: hard borders (2px/3px/4px black), solid fills, hard drop shadows (#000 offset).
- Restraint and self-critique: cut redundant elements, ensure clean responsiveness down to mobile.
